## How to apply

- **Prospective Ph.D. students:** email [mou00006@umn.edu](mailto:mou00006@umn.edu) with your CV, transcripts and a short statement of research interests, and apply to the [UMN ISE graduate program](https://cse.umn.edu/isye).
- **Current UMN students:** undergraduate and master's students interested in research are welcome to reach out.
- **Remote research assistants:** we welcome remote collaborators; please email with your CV.

Due to the large volume of inquiries we receive, we are not able to reply to every email individually. We will contact you if there is a good fit.
