The Mou Group develops learning, optimization and computational methods for complex engineering systems, with the goal of improving their quality, productivity and efficiency. Our work draws on modern machine learning (modeling), large-scale optimization (algorithms) and computational science (efficient implementation), with applications in vision-based industrial inspection, advanced manufacturing, energy systems and more.
